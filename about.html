I'm an undergraduate at Princeton University who likes building things that work in the real world — whether that's a rocket's recovery system, an assistive device for someone who needs one, or a model that makes sense of a messy dataset.

My work spans hands-on mechanical design, quantitative research, and team leadership. On the engineering side, I lead recovery systems development for Princeton Rocketry and founded Empowered Innovations, a nonprofit that designs and 3D-prints assistive devices. On the research side, I've worked on climate data analysis, materials and battery-chemistry modeling, and biology research on neurodegeneration in Drosophila melanogaster models. Outside of engineering, I direct programming for a couple of Princeton student organizations and edit for The Daily Princetonian.

What I'm working on

Right now: recovery system design for Princeton Rocketry, undergraduate research at Princeton, and data analytics work on EV/battery materials at Orbis-Metallicum.

Beyond engineering

I play on the club baseball team and I'm a huge Mets fan (LGM!). When I'm not in the lab or the shop, I'm probably hiking, at the gym, or just trying to be outside as much as possible.
